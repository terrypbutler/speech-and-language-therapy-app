# SLT authenticity and quality review

Use this checklist before a synthetic case is used with learners. Record names, dates, decisions and required changes in the organisation's approved review system. This file is a development aid, not evidence of approval.

## Clinical and educational review

- Confirm the encounter, communication/swallowing context, priorities, time course and escalation placeholders are plausible for the learner level and setting.
- Confirm every finding, prerequisite, consequence and visible cue is educator-authored and locally appropriate.
- Confirm learning outcomes, prebrief, fidelity limitations, facilitation plan and debrief align.
- Confirm the case cannot be mistaken for diagnosis, assessment, food/fluid guidance, treatment advice or automatic competence assessment.

## Client authenticity and lived experience

- Review the client's goals, language, multimodal communication, hesitations, refusals and non-verbal palette with relevant lived-experience reviewers.
- Remove stereotypes, unnecessary demographic cues and behaviour included only to make the learner's task easier.
- Ensure communication differences are not treated as evidence of cognition, capacity, compliance or lack of insight.
- For child cases, ensure assent is active and the child's perspective is not displaced by an adult's account.

## Accessibility, equality and inclusion

- Test keyboard-only use, focus order, labels, contrast, zoom and screen-reader announcements.
- Review language load, reading level, processing time and whether the interface disadvantages learners using assistive technology or an additional language.
- Review names, identities, family structures, disability, age, culture and faith for representational balance and stereotype risk.
- Provide a supported way to pause, opt out and re-enter learning after distress.

## AI and information governance

- Use synthetic data only. Never enter identifiable client or learner information.
- Confirm provider, region, retention, access controls, contracts and local DPIA requirements before enabling an API.
- Confirm AI receives only allow-listed revealed state and cannot change findings, diagnosis, consent, assent, recommendations or event timing.
- Test malformed output, provider failure, unsafe recommendations, prompt injection, hallucinated facts and authored fallback behaviour.

## Pilot, calibration and release

- Pilot with novice and experienced learners, at least two facilitators, relevant specialists and lived-experience reviewers.
- Compare facilitator ratings with AI-supported feedback; treat disagreements as calibration evidence, never as automatic learner failure.
- Test direct, supportive, tangential, ambiguous and concerning actions, including reasonable actions outside the authored pathway.
- Record case version, owner, intended learners, supervision level, reviewers, limitations, approval decision, review date and evidence of retesting.
